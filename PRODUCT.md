# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Most visitors arrive from the link in Marco's Instagram or TikTok bio (@marco_founder), on a phone, often in the evening, scrolling casually. They decide within a minute whether he is legit, good, and worth messaging. Three groups:

- **Small-business owners** who need a website or an AI automation and want someone who will just handle it. Non-technical; judge by the work and the vibe.
- **Tech and security leads** looking for a penetration test, red team engagement, or OSINT work. Technical; judge by credentials and verifiable proof.
- **Founders and peers** who followed him on social and want to see what he builds.

The job: see the work, understand what he offers, and slide into his DMs.

## Product Purpose

A portfolio that converts social followers into clients. Success is a visitor tapping through to Instagram or TikTok to message him, or emailing him, after seeing at least one project. It also has to work as a link he can drop in a DM to prove credibility.

## Brand Personality

Cinematic, confident, playful. Anime title-card energy grounded by real work: a founder who builds websites and breaks into networks, and has fun doing both. Voice is first person, direct, short sentences, no corporate filler.

Emotional goal: "this person is sharp and I want to work with them," landing within the first viewport.

## Anti-references

- Generic dev-portfolio starters: centred hero, skill-badge grids, identical project cards, "Let's work together" boilerplate.
- Hacker clichés: green-on-black terminals, Matrix rain, Anonymous masks, skull iconography.
- Copied anime: no existing characters, logos, series typography, or screenshots. The aesthetic is original.
- Neon cyberpunk overload: purple/cyan glow everywhere. The palette is sunset ember, used sparingly.

## Design Principles

1. **Work first.** Every section earns its place by pointing toward the projects or the DM button.
2. **Title-card moments, quiet reading.** Headings get the cinematic entrance; body copy stays calm and legible.
3. **Phone is the main stage.** Social traffic is mobile. Desktop gets extra spectacle (pinned scroll), never extra content.
4. **Proof over claims.** Verify links, live sites, real numbers. If it can't be shown, say so plainly.
5. **Motion is optional.** Everything reads perfectly with motion off.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.2 AA. Full keyboard support with a visible neon focus ring. `prefers-reduced-motion` disables smooth scroll, pinning, the ember canvas and large entrances. Decorative katakana and textures are `aria-hidden`. Lighthouse 90+ on mobile for Performance, Accessibility, Best Practices and SEO.
