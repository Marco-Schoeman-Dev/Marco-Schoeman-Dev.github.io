# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Most visitors arrive from the link in Marco's Instagram or TikTok bio (@marco_founder), often straight after a Live, on a phone, in the evening. They decide within a minute whether he is worth their time. Three groups:

- **Founders, C-suite and creators** who watched a Live. They want the write-up, then a reason to apply to The Founders Club.
- **Small-business owners** who need a website or an AI automation and want someone who will just handle it. Non-technical; judge by the work.
- **Business and IT leads** looking for a penetration test, Active Directory red team or OSINT. Judge by credentials and plain language.

The job: read a Live, read another, apply to the Club. Or see the work and start a project.

## Product Purpose

One site for Marco's personal brand and The Founders Club (application-only peer network, USD $30/month for the first 20 founding members, then $40). Success, in order: a Club application; a second Live read; a project enquiry by DM or email. It also has to work as a link he can drop in a DM to prove credibility.

Never link to the payment platform. Approved members get a private payment link from Marco. Name no clients beyond the three portfolio projects; no legal advice.

## Brand Personality

Cinematic visuals, deadpan words. Voice: sarcastic, observational, logical, short. First person. Not goofy, no hype words (no "unlock", "elevate", "game-changing"), no corporate filler. Brand name is always "The Founders Club".

Emotional goal: "this person is sharp and I want to work with them," landing within the first viewport.

## Anti-references

- Generic dev-portfolio starters: centred hero, skill-badge grids, identical project cards, "Let's work together" boilerplate.
- Hacker clichés: green-on-black terminals, Matrix rain, Anonymous masks, skull iconography.
- Copied anime: no existing characters, logos, series typography, or screenshots. The aesthetic is original.
- Neon city: tube glows, purple/cyan, cyberpunk signage, Japanese labels. The palette is sunset ember, used sparingly.
- Guru marketing: countdown timers, "limited spots!!", income screenshots, motivational quotes.

## Design Principles

1. **One next step per page.** Home → Lives or Apply. Live → Apply, or More Lives. Club → Apply. Work → Start a project.
2. **Title-card moments, quiet reading.** Headings get the cinematic entrance; body copy stays calm and legible.
3. **Phone is the main stage.** Social traffic is mobile. Desktop gets extra spectacle (pinned scroll), never extra content.
4. **Proof over claims.** Verify links, live sites, real numbers. If it can't be shown, say so plainly.
5. **Motion is optional.** Everything reads perfectly with motion off.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.2 AA. Full keyboard support with a visible ember focus ring. `prefers-reduced-motion` disables smooth scroll, the ember canvas and large entrances. Decorative textures are `aria-hidden`. Lighthouse 90+ on mobile for Performance, Accessibility, Best Practices and SEO.
